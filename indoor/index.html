Indoor guide

Hitting indoors

A garage or simulator room works well. Light matters more than anything else: get the ball's path bright and most missed shots go away.

  • Light the ball's path
  • Give the ball room
  • iPhone or iPad behind the ball
  • Use white balls
  • Play a course
  • Troubleshooting indoors

1. Light the ball's path

Indoor light is much dimmer than daylight, even when the room looks bright. More light on the ball means fewer missed shots.

  • Hang a light straight above the ball. A ceiling track light with its heads aimed straight down works well. Aim one head at the ball and another a few feet further along its flight. A plug-in track light is easiest: it plugs into a normal outlet, so there's no wiring. This is the one we use.
  • Light the path, not just the tee. Keep the first few feet of the ball's flight bright too.
  • Window light and ordinary room lights aren't enough on their own.

2. Give the ball room

Leave at least 8 ft (2.4 m) from the ball to your screen or net. 10 ft (3 m) is comfortable.

3. iPhone or iPad behind the ball, same as outdoors

Put your iPhone or iPad on a tripod 7.5 ft (2.3 m) directly behind the ball, on the line from the ball to the screen. That's two driver lengths.

Measure it. Every speed and carry is scaled by this distance, indoors as much as out. See Get the distance right.

4. Use white balls

White balls track best indoors.

Play a course

Once your shots read reliably, send them to a course. Open Play Course on a second iPad or iPhone and cable it to your projector, or send your shots to an Open Connect simulator like Muni Golf. Pick either in Simulator on the device tracking the ball.

Troubleshooting indoors

"Too dark" or "No ball found"
Add light above the ball, or aim the light you have straight down at it.
Fast shots go missing, slow ones read fine
Aim a second light further along the ball's path, and check there are at least 8 ft to the screen.
Numbers look off
Measure the distance from your iPhone or iPad to the ball. Every number is scaled by it.

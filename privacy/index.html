HitSum Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

HitSum is a golf app published by Kiki Gadgets Ltd ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information HitSum collects, why, who handles it, and the choices you have. We keep collection to what the app needs to work.

The short version

  • You can use HitSum without an account. Signing in with Apple is optional.
  • As a guest, your swing videos, shots and history stay on your device.
  • If you sign in, your shot history is backed up to your account so it comes back on a new device. Your swing videos are never uploaded. They stay in your photo library.
  • If you host a shared session, its code and shot numbers are stored online so your friends can watch.
  • If you connect HitSum to golf simulator software, your shots go straight to your own computer over your local network, not to us.
  • Usage statistics and crash reports are on by default, and you can turn them off in Settings.
  • We don't sell your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Permissions HitSum asks for

HitSum asks iOS for these permissions, and only uses each one for the reason given:

  • Camera: to record your swing at high frame rate and find the ball.
  • Motion & Fitness: to read your device's tilt and the air pressure while recording, so the camera angle and the air the ball flies through don't have to be guessed.
  • Photos (add only): to save a copy of each swing you record to your photo library.
  • Photos (full access, when you ask for it): to open swings you've already saved, and to find your swings' videos again on a new device.
  • Local Network (when you connect a simulator): to send each shot to golf simulator software on your computer.

You can change any of these at any time in your device's Settings → HitSum. HitSum does not use your location or your microphone.

Information that stays on your device

The swing videos HitSum records, and anything it reads from your photo library, are processed on your device. We never receive your videos or photos. They leave your device only when you export or share them yourself, for example by sending a traced video or a share card, or through your own iCloud Photos backup, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.

If you use HitSum as a guest, your shot measurements, session history and club analysis also stay only on your device. Spoken results are generated on your device.

Information we collect

If you sign in with Apple (optional). We receive the account identifier Apple provides and, if you choose to share them, your name and email address (Apple may give us a private relay address instead of your real email). We use these only to operate your account. HitSum works fully as a guest without them.

Your shot history, when you're signed in. While you're signed in, HitSum backs up your history to your account so it appears on every device you sign in to and comes back if you reinstall. This includes:

  • each launch monitor shot: ball speed, launch, direction, carry and similar numbers, the club, the time of the shot, and technical details of how it was measured, such as camera settings, the ball's tracked positions and air conditions;
  • each traced swing's record: its trace and measurements, its date and length, and identifiers for its video in your photo library, so HitSum can find the video again on another device. The video itself is not uploaded.

Shots and swings you recorded as a guest on a device are added to your account when you first sign in on it. When you delete a shot or swing, it's deleted from your account and your other devices too. Your app preferences are not backed up.

If you host a Join session. When you share a live session, we store the session code, the shots you hit during that session (the same kind of shot details listed above), and your account identifier, so people you give the code to can follow along. Joining someone else's session doesn't store anything about you.

Usage and crash data. Unless you turn it off (Settings → Preferences → Share usage and crash reports), HitSum sends:

  • anonymous usage statistics, such as which screens are opened and whether shots were measured successfully, through Firebase Analytics. These are not linked to your identity.
  • crash reports, through Firebase Crashlytics, so we can fix problems. If you're signed in when a crash happens, the report includes your account identifier.

Purchases. If you subscribe to HitSum Premium, Apple processes your payment. We never see your card or payment details. Apple and our subscription service, RevenueCat, record your purchase and subscription status so the app knows what you've unlocked. RevenueCat uses an anonymous identifier until you sign in, and your account identifier after that.

Golf simulator connection

If you turn on a simulator connection, HitSum sends each shot's numbers (such as ball speed, launch, direction, spin and club) directly to the simulator software at the address you choose on your local network. This data goes from your device to your computer and never passes through our servers. What the simulator software does with it is covered by that software's own privacy terms.

How we use information

We use it only to:

  • provide HitSum's features, including sign-in, history backup and sync, shared sessions and Premium;
  • understand how the app is used and fix crashes;
  • answer you when you contact us.

We don't sell your information, share it with advertisers, or use it to track you across other apps or websites.

How long we keep information

  • Account, backed-up history and hosted sessions: until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Crash reports and usage statistics: kept by Firebase for a limited period under its retention settings. Usage statistics are not linked to you.
  • Purchase records: kept by Apple and RevenueCat as needed for billing, refunds, accounting and legal requirements.
  • Data on your device: stays until you delete it or remove the app.

Your choices and rights

  • Use HitSum without an account by continuing as a guest. Nothing is backed up.
  • Turn off usage and crash reporting in Settings → Preferences.
  • Change permissions at any time in your device's Settings → HitSum.
  • Delete your account in Settings → Account → Delete account. This removes your sign-in, your backed-up history and every session you hosted. The shots and videos already on your devices, and the videos in your photo library, are not deleted. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription, because Apple bills it: cancel in your Apple ID's Subscriptions settings.
  • Revoke Sign in with Apple at any time in your device's Settings → [your name] → Sign in with Apple.
  • Ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, using the contact details below. Depending on where you live (for example the UK, the EU, or California), you may have further rights under local law, and we'll honour them.

Children

HitSum is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it on this page and change the effective date above. If a change is significant, we'll also tell you in the app.
